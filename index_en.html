1 Information and Usage Data We Collect

PicTale does not require account registration or login. To provide, improve, and operate the service reliably, we may automatically collect or process the information listed below.

A. Identifiers

Item Collection context Purpose
Advertising ID (GAID) In builds that include ads and when Google AdMob is used Ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention
App instance ID / Firebase Installation ID Firebase Analytics, Remote Config, and Installations Usage analytics, configuration delivery, and service improvement
Crashlytics installation identifier Firebase Crashlytics Distinguishing and analyzing crash and error reports

B. Usage Analytics and App Activity

  • Events: story and report creation, AI model downloads, ad viewing, sharing, notification settings, onboarding progress, and similar actions
  • Event parameters: app language, inference backend (GPU/CPU), model version, inference duration, and similar technical values
  • Automatically collected data: first launch, sessions, access frequency, user engagement, retention, and similar metrics
Content is not included in analytics events. We collect only event names and technical status values. Photos, story text, titles, notes, and tags are not sent to Firebase Analytics.

C. Device and App Environment Information

Firebase and advertising SDKs may automatically collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, country, locale, app version, and screen information.

D. Crash and Performance Information

  • Error and crash stack traces
  • App and device state at the time of an error
  • Custom diagnostic keys such as inference backend and model version

E. Photo Location Information

If a selected photo contains GPS metadata and you grant the relevant permission, PicTale may read the GPS coordinates from the photo’s EXIF metadata and convert them into a human-readable place name.

Important information about location conversion PicTale uses Android Geocoder to convert coordinates into a place name. Depending on the device and service environment, the coordinates may be sent to Google’s geocoding service. The photo itself is not transmitted; only the coordinates may be sent.

F. Network Connection Information

When downloading AI models from Hugging Face or communicating with services such as Firebase and AdMob, network information including your IP address, request time, and request details may be transmitted to those service providers.

2 Information not collected

PicTale processes core content on-device. The information below is processed on your device and is not transmitted to PicTale’s servers, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or advertising SDKs.
Photos and videos Story text Titles Notes and tags Pet names Pet profiles Location notes

Because PicTale does not provide account registration or login, we do not collect member information used to directly identify you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3 Purposes of Processing

  • Analyzing feature usage and improving the service
  • Analyzing errors, crashes, and performance issues to improve stability
  • Delivering ads, controlling ad frequency, and preventing fraud
  • Displaying a human-readable place name based on photo GPS coordinates
  • Providing AI models and app configuration files

We use collected information only for the purposes described above. If those purposes change, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by applicable law.

4 Retention Period

Category Retention period Notes
Firebase Analytics events Up to 90 days Subject to the actual retention configuration in the Firebase console
Firebase Crashlytics information Up to 90 days Deleted after the diagnostic purpose is fulfilled or according to the provider’s policy
Advertising-related information According to Google advertising policies and settings May be affected by advertising ID resets and device settings

PicTale does not operate user accounts. Deleting the app removes locally stored app data and breaks the association with the previous app installation identifier. Information already processed by external service providers may remain for a period determined by each provider’s retention policy.

5 Service Providers and International Transfers

PicTale uses the external services listed below to operate the app. In this process, information may be processed on servers located outside the Republic of Korea.

Service provider Service used Transferred or processed data Purpose Location and transfer method
Google LLC Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Installations, AdMob, and Geocoding Installation identifiers, app activity, device information, error information, advertising ID, location coordinates, IP address, and request information Analytics, error diagnostics, configuration delivery, ad delivery, and location-name conversion Transferred over the network to Google data-processing locations, including the United States, when the service is used
Hugging Face, Inc. AI model file downloads IP address and request information Providing and downloading AI models Transferred to overseas servers when a model download is requested

The retention period for internationally transferred information follows this Policy and each service provider’s policy. You may limit certain processing as described in “Your Rights and Opt-Out Options” below.

6 Your Rights and Opt-Out Options

Analytics
Disable usage analytics
You can disable usage analytics under More → Settings → Data Collection in the app.
Crash
Crash reporting
Crashlytics information is collected continuously to maintain app stability and respond to errors, and no separate toggle is provided. Photos and story text are not included in crash reports.
Location
Limit use of photo location information
GPS coordinates are not used if you deny photo-location access or select a photo without location metadata. You can change this permission at any time in Android’s photo location access settings.
Ads
Control the advertising identifier
You can delete or reset the advertising ID and change ad-personalization choices in your Android device’s advertising privacy or ad settings.
Delete
Delete app data and disconnect the installation identifier
Deleting the app removes PicTale’s locally stored data from the device and breaks the association with the app installation identifier.

7 Automatic Collection Technologies

PicTale uses third-party SDKs, including Firebase SDKs and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. These SDKs may automatically collect information related to app launches, usage events, installation identifiers, device environment, errors, and advertising.

  • Firebase Analytics: collection of app usage analytics and automatic events
  • Firebase Crashlytics: collection of crashes, errors, and diagnostic information
  • Firebase Remote Config and Installations: delivery of app configuration and installation-level identification
  • Google AdMob: ad delivery, frequency control, and fraud prevention

8 Security Measures

  • Processing core content such as photos and stories on-device whenever possible
  • Restricting analytics events and error logs from recording content or direct identifiers
  • Using encrypted communications such as HTTPS when communicating with external services
  • Collecting only the minimum information required to operate the service
  • Periodically reviewing external SDKs and processed data categories

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Policy changes, we will provide notice of the changes and effective date through app update notes, an in-app notice, or this page. If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide advance notice within a reasonable period before it takes effect.
